Orbitlog

Orbitlog is a personal, non-commercial tool that its owner runs on their own computer to see statistics for their own social media accounts: follower and following counts, how those change over time, and how their own posts perform.

How it uses TikTok

The owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and Orbitlog reads, through the TikTok Display API, the signed-in account’s basic profile (display name, avatar, username), its follower, following and video counts, and the list of its own public videos with their like, comment, share and view counts. It does not post, edit or delete anything on TikTok.

Orbitlog is not offered to the public. Only the owner’s own TikTok accounts are connected.
