Privacy Policy

Orbitlog is a personal tool used only by its owner, with the owner’s own accounts.

What is collected

When the owner connects a TikTok account, Orbitlog reads that account’s display name, avatar, username, follower count, following count, video count, and its own public videos with their like, comment, share and view counts. Access tokens issued by TikTok are stored so the data can be refreshed.

Where it is stored

All data is stored in a local database on the owner’s own computer. Access tokens are encrypted. Nothing is uploaded to any server operated by Orbitlog, and there is no such server.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to anyone.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept until the owner deletes it. Disconnecting an account in Orbitlog deletes its access token and asks TikTok to revoke it; deleting the account in Orbitlog removes all stored data for it. Access can also be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the issue tracker of the repository that hosts this page.
